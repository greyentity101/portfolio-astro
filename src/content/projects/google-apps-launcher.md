A fast, offline-capable desktop app giving instant access to 24 Google services. Search, filter, and pin favorites — all powered by a lightweight Electron shell with persistent local storage.

Features:
- Search across all Google apps with instant filtering
- Favorite/pin apps with localStorage persistence
- Electron-native shell with system integration
- Keyboard-driven: ⌘K command palette
- Privacy-first: no tracking, no telemetry
